About

I’m a Cloud and DevOps Engineer in Chennai. My whole career so far has been inside Tagit, a product fintech company whose Mobeix platform powers retail banking apps for institutions across several markets — which means every pipeline I touch ends up in front of people checking their balance on a Monday morning.

That context shaped how I work. Banking releases can’t be casual, so I spend my time making delivery repeatable and auditable: standardised GitLab pipelines instead of per-team snowflakes, artifacts promoted through Nexus rather than copied by hand, and security scanning that runs whether or not anyone remembers to ask for it.

I started as a trainee in 2024 writing YAML for a handful of repositories. Since then I’ve migrated the platform off Jenkins, taken on the on-prem Kubernetes estate, and most recently built an AI code review bot that comments on merge requests before a human opens them. Two promotions in that time.
