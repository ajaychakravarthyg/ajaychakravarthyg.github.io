About

Summary

I’m a DevOps Engineer with 2.5+ years of hands-on experience in CI/CD automation at Tagit, a product-based fintech company. Banks use its Mobeix platform to run their digital banking apps.

I build and improve GitLab CI/CD pipelines for microservices, ship containers with Docker and Kubernetes, and run the AWS infrastructure behind our CI (EC2, ECR, ELB, Aurora). Security checks are part of every pipeline I build, not something added at the end.

I also help keep production running. I troubleshoot production servers, lead root cause analysis (RCA) after incidents, set up the monitoring alerts, and take on-call shifts for production, pre-prod and SIT environments. I coordinate releases with our development teams and with bank customers, from build to production rollout.
